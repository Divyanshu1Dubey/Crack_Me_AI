# Medical Current Affairs 2024-2025 — UPSC CMS Interview Ready

## National Medical Commission (NMC) Reforms

### Key Developments
- **NMC replaced MCI** (Medical Council of India) under the NMC Act 2019, fully operational from 2020.
- **NEXT (National Exit Test)**: Proposed single exam replacing NEET-PG for PG admissions and license to practice. Implementation repeatedly delayed; latest update indicates phased rollout from 2025-26.
- **Community Health Provider (CHP)**: NMC's proposal for mid-level health practitioners at Sub-Health Centres, controversial among medical professionals.
- **Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs)**: New FMGE screening test regulations — mandatory qualifying exam before clinical practice in India.
- **MBBS Curriculum Reform**: Competency-Based Medical Education (CBME) curriculum fully implemented across all medical colleges from 2019 batch onwards.

## Ayushman Bharat Updates (2024-2025)

### PM-JAY Performance
- **Coverage**: Over 30 crore Ayushman cards issued as of 2024.
- **Hospital Empanelment**: 28,000+ hospitals (public + private) empanelled.
- **Treatment Packages**: 1,949+ treatment packages covering surgeries, day care, diagnostics.
- **Budget 2024-25**: ₹7,500 crore allocation for PM-JAY.
- **Extension**: Coverage expanded to include all senior citizens above 70 years (announced Budget 2024).
- **Challenges**: Delayed reimbursements to hospitals, fraudulent claims, uneven urban-rural access.

### Health & Wellness Centres (AB-HWC)
- **Target**: 1.5 lakh HWCs by 2025 (achieved ~1.6 lakh by mid-2024).
- **Services**: 12 comprehensive primary healthcare services including free medicines.
- **CPHC**: Comprehensive Primary Health Care model with NCD screening.

## NEET PG 2024-2025 Changes

- **NEET PG 2024**: Exam held in August 2024 after multiple delays.
- **New Pattern**: Computer-based test, reduced from 300 to 200 questions.
- **Cutoff Trends**: General category cutoffs showing upward trend.
- **Counselling**: Centralized NEET PG counselling through MCC, state counselling through respective directorates.
- **Seat Increase**: ~14,000 new PG seats added between 2020-2024.
- **EWS Reservation**: 10% EWS reservation upheld by Supreme Court, applicable in all-India quota.

## WHO Guidelines Updates

### Cardiovascular Disease Prevention (2023-24)
- Updated risk assessment tools for low-resource settings.
- Polypill strategy endorsed for high-risk CVD prevention.
- Revised hypertension thresholds: ≥140/90 for diagnosis, ≥130/80 for high-risk populations.

### Tuberculosis (2024)
- Updated BPaL regimen (Bedaquiline-Pretomanid-Linezolid) for XDR-TB.
- India's TB elimination target: 2025 (5 years ahead of global SDG target).
- New rapid molecular tests for drug resistance detection.

### Malaria
- RTS,S/AS01 (Mosquirix) vaccine — first malaria vaccine WHO-recommended (2021), deployed in Africa.
- R21/Matrix-M vaccine approved by WHO (2023) — higher efficacy, easier manufacturing.
- India targeting malaria elimination by 2030.

## National Health Policy Updates

### National Health Policy 2017 — Key Targets
- Health expenditure to 2.5% of GDP by 2025 (currently ~2.1%).
- Reduce IMR to 28 (achieved), MMR to 100 (near target).
- Universal Health Coverage through PM-JAY and HWCs.

### Digital Health Mission (ABDM)
- **ABHA Number**: Unique 14-digit health ID for every citizen.
- **Health Information Exchange**: Interoperable health records.
- **Unified Health Interface (UHI)**: Like UPI for healthcare services.
- **Health Facility Registry (HFR)**: Digital registry of all health facilities.
- **Integration**: ABHA linked with CoWIN, PM-JAY, and government hospital systems.

## Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR)

### India's National Action Plan on AMR (NAP-AMR)
- **Launched**: 2017, aligned with WHO Global Action Plan.
- **Key Pillars**: Awareness, surveillance, infection prevention, antibiotic stewardship, research.
- **ICMR Surveillance**: Network of 30+ AMR surveillance labs across India.
- **Red Line Campaign**: "Antibiotics with red line — Not to be sold without prescription."
- **DCGI Orders**: Prescription-only status for higher antibiotics (carbapenems, colistin).

## Recent Disease Outbreaks & Responses

### Nipah Virus (Kerala, 2023)
- Limited outbreak in Kozhikode district.
- Swift containment through contact tracing and isolation.
- No vaccine available; ribavirin used empirically.

### Chandipura Virus (Gujarat, 2024)
- Outbreak primarily affecting children.
- Spread through sandfly vectors.
- High case fatality rate; supportive care only.

### Zika Virus
- Sporadic cases reported in multiple Indian states (2024).
- Focus on Aedes mosquito control and pregnancy surveillance.

## Mental Health

### Mental Healthcare Act 2017
- Every person has a right to access mental healthcare.
- Attempt to suicide decriminalized (Section 309 IPC).
- Advance directive provision for mental health treatment.
- District Mental Health Programme (DMHP) under NHM strengthened.

### NIMHANS Study (2024)
- Prevalence of mental disorders in India: ~10.6% of population.
- Depression and anxiety most common; treatment gap > 80%.
- Tele-MANAS helpline: 14416 (launched 2022, operational in 36 states).

## Medical Education Updates

### New Medical Colleges (2024)
- Total MBBS seats in India: ~1,12,000 (2024).
- ~350 new medical colleges added since 2014.
- Government medical colleges: 55% of total seats.
- NMC minimum standards for medical college establishment updated.

### CBME Implementation
- Longitudinal integration of clinical exposure from first year.
- AETCOM (Attitude, Ethics and Communication) module mandatory.
- Foundation course for incoming medical students.
- Formative and summative assessment balance.

# Content Strategy — CrackCMS

**Date:** July 21, 2026

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Exam Guides** — comprehensive long-form guides per exam (UPSC CMS, NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE, USMLE, Medical Officer).
2. **Subject Deep-Dives** — anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, pharmacology, pathology (Pinterest-style infographics + 2,000-word articles).
3. **PYQ Analysis** — weekly deep-dives on the hardest PYQs.
4. **Topper Stories** — interview series with UPSC CMS / NEET PG toppers.
5. **Books & Resources** — book reviews, comparison guides, "best books for X" lists.
6. **AI in Medicine** — thought leadership on LLMs in medical education.
7. **Career Guidance** — government doctor jobs, salary comparisons, post-MBBS paths.

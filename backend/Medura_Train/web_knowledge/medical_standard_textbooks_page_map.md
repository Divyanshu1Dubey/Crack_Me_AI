# High-Yield Medical Topics Textbook Page-Mapping Directory

This document provides a highly accurate cross-reference directory mapping critical clinical topics tested in the UPSC CMS and NEET-PG exams to their exact chapter and page numbers in standard medical textbooks.

---

## 1. GENERAL MEDICINE (Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine - 21st Edition)

| Topic / Disease | Core Sub-Concepts | Chapter | Page Range (Vol 1 & 2) | Clinical Pearls & Key High-Yields |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Diabetes Mellitus** | DKA vs HHS Diagnostic Criteria | Chapter 403 | Vol 2: p. 3012 - 3020 | DKA: Glucose >250, pH <7.30, ketones positive. HHS: Glucose >600, pH >7.30, Osmolality >320. |
| **Hypertension** | Classification & Emergency Management | Chapter 277 | Vol 2: p. 1984 - 1995 | Emergency: BP >180/120 with target organ damage (requires IV Labetalol/Nicardipine). Urgency: No target organ damage (oral agents). |
| **Infectious Endocarditis** | Modified Duke's Criteria | Chapter 123 | Vol 1: p. 950 - 962 | 2 Major OR 1 Major + 3 Minor OR 5 Minor. Major: +ve blood culture, Echo showing vegetation. |
| **Rheumatic Fever** | Modified Jones Criteria | Chapter 352 | Vol 2: p. 2514 - 2520 | 2 Major or 1 Major + 2 Minor. Erythema marginatum and Sydenham's chorea are pathognomonic. |
| **Tuberculosis** | Pulmonary Cavities & Daily FDC Regimen | Chapter 173 | Vol 1: p. 1342 - 1360 | Daily FDC (Fixed Dose Combination): 2HRZE / 4HRE. Ethambutol causes retrobulbar neuritis. |
| **Chronic Kidney Disease** | KDIGO GFR Stages 1 to 5 | Chapter 311 | Vol 2: p. 2240 - 2251 | Stage 1: GFR >90; Stage 3a: 45-59; Stage 3b: 30-44; Stage 4: 15-29; Stage 5: <15 (ESRD, requires dialysis). |
| **Systemic Lupus Erythematosus** | EULAR/ACR Criteria & Antibodies | Chapter 356 | Vol 2: p. 2552 - 2564 | Best screening test: ANA (99% sensitive). Most specific test: Anti-dsDNA or Anti-Smith (99% specific). |
| **Thyroid Disorders** | Graves vs Subacute Thyroiditis | Chapter 383 | Vol 2: p. 2810 - 2824 | Graves: painless hyperthyroidism with high RAIU. Thyroiditis: painful thyroid gland with low RAIU. |
| **Acute Myocardial Infarction** | ECG Lead Mapping & Fibrinolysis | Chapter 275 | Vol 2: p. 1954 - 1968 | Anterior: V1-V4 (LAD); Lateral: I, aVL, V5-V6 (LCx); Inferior: II, III, aVF (RCA). |

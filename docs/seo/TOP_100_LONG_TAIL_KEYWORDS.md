# Top 100 Long-Tail Keywords — CrackCMS

> High-intent, low-competition, mostly **informational + transactional** long-tail queries. Volumes are best-effort IN/EN estimates. Each row is paired with the CrackCMS page that should capture it.

---

## Why long-tail matters here

UPSC CMS, NEET PG, INI-CET, FMGE, USMLE and Medical Officer queries cluster into **3 intent buckets**:
- "I want to qualify" (informational + commercial): pattern, syllabus, eligibility, books.
- "I need to practice" (transactional): mock test, PYQ, MCQ, free quiz.
- "I'm comparing / deciding" (comparative): CMS vs INI-CET, USMLE vs NEXT.

These long-tails convert significantly higher than head terms.

| "image-based" | Specific guide (e.g. NEET PG image-based Qs) |

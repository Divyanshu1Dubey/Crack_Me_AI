## 11. Search-intent Distribution (recommendation)

Target the next 90 days of content production at:

| Intent | Effort weight |
|---|---|
| Informational (definitions, eligibility, books) | 50% |
| Transactional (free mock, registration, premium plans) | 25% |
| Comparative / Comparison | 15% |
| Navigational (brand → page) | 10% |

This split aligns with the SERP landscape for medical exam queries in India, where most informational queries dominate but transactional volume is high in the immediate pre-exam months.

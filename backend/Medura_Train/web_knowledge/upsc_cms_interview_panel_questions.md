# UPSC CMS Interview Panel — Frequently Asked Questions

## Category 1: Medical Knowledge & Clinical Scenarios

1. What is the initial management of a patient with acute myocardial infarction in a PHC setting?
2. How would you manage a case of severe malaria in a remote district hospital?
3. Describe the management protocol for a patient with diabetic ketoacidosis.
4. What are the WHO guidelines for management of severe acute malnutrition?
5. How do you manage a case of eclampsia in a peripheral health center?
6. What is the role of a medical officer in a mass casualty incident?
7. Describe the national tuberculosis elimination program (NTEP) — what are the key changes from RNTCP?
8. How would you manage a snake bite case when anti-venom is unavailable?
9. What is the recommended treatment protocol for dengue hemorrhagic fever?
10. Describe the principles of rational drug therapy and how you'd implement them.
11. What are the red flag signs in a pediatric patient that require immediate referral?
12. How do you approach a case of unexplained fever lasting more than 2 weeks?
13. What is the difference between Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes management in a primary care setting?
14. Describe the complete immunization schedule for children under 5 years as per NIS.
15. How would you set up an isolation ward during an outbreak of a communicable disease?

## Category 2: Public Health & Community Medicine

16. What is the role of a CMS medical officer in implementing the National Health Mission?
17. Describe Ayushman Bharat scheme — how does it work and what are its components?
18. What is your understanding of the National Medical Commission (NMC) Act?
19. How would you organize a pulse polio campaign in your district?
20. What are the key indicators used to assess the health status of a community?
21. Describe the ASHA worker program and its impact on maternal and child health.
22. What steps would you take to control an outbreak of cholera in your jurisdiction?
23. Explain the concept of Primary Health Care and its relevance to Indian healthcare.
24. What is the Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) related to health, and how is India performing?
25. How would you promote family planning services in a rural area?

## Category 3: Ethics & Situational Questions

26. A patient refuses blood transfusion on religious grounds but needs it urgently. What do you do?
27. You discover that a colleague is prescribing unnecessary expensive drugs. How do you handle this?
28. A political leader pressures you to provide a false medical certificate. Your response?
29. How would you handle a situation where the patient's family demands euthanasia?
30. You witness a senior doctor committing medical negligence. What are your ethical obligations?
31. A patient dies during surgery. How do you communicate with the family?
32. What is informed consent and when can treatment proceed without it?
33. Describe the principles of medical ethics — autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, justice.
34. A tribal community refuses vaccination. How would you approach this?
35. How do you maintain patient confidentiality when dealing with HIV-positive patients?

## Category 4: Administrative & Service-Related

36. What are the duties and responsibilities of a CMS medical officer?
37. Describe the career progression in Central Health Services.
38. What is the difference between CHS and state health services?
39. How would you manage the human resources at a district hospital?
40. What is the role of telemedicine in modern healthcare delivery?
41. How would you implement electronic health records in a government hospital?
42. Describe the Drug Controller General of India (DCGI) — role and functions.
43. What is clinical governance and how would you implement it in your hospital?
44. How do you deal with staff absenteeism in a government healthcare facility?
45. What steps would you take to reduce Out-of-Pocket (OOP) expenditure on health?

## Category 5: Current Affairs & Policy

46. What is the National Digital Health Mission and its significance?
47. Describe the impact of COVID-19 on India's healthcare infrastructure.
48. What are the recent changes in the NEET-PG examination pattern?
49. What is the One Health approach and why is it important?
50. Describe the PM-JAY (Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana) scheme.
51. What is the role of public-private partnership in healthcare?
52. How has digital technology transformed healthcare delivery in India?
53. What is antimicrobial resistance and what steps is India taking to combat it?
54. Describe India's vaccine manufacturing capabilities and their global significance.
55. What are the key provisions of the Clinical Establishments Act?

## Category 6: Personality Assessment & Motivation

56. Why did you choose CMS over private practice?
57. Where do you see yourself in 10 years within CHS?
58. Tell us about a challenging clinical case you managed and what you learned.
59. What is your greatest strength and weakness as a doctor?
60. How would you balance administrative duties with clinical practice?
61. Describe a situation where you showed leadership in a medical setting.
62. What qualities make a good public health administrator?
63. How do you handle stress and burnout in medical practice?
64. What books/journals do you read for continuing medical education?
65. If posted in a remote area with minimal resources, how would you cope?
